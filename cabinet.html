03 / PHYSICAL CUSTODY

The four-cabinet system

Four LOTO cabinets are located together so keys and lockout boxes always have designated positions.

HV KEYS

Cabinet 1

HV

Dedicated HV isolation-lock keys, each on its numbered hook.

LV KEYS

Cabinet 2

LV

Dedicated LV isolation-lock keys, each on its numbered hook.

LOCKOUT BOXES

Cabinet 3

LB

All numbered lockout boxes, each returned to its assigned position after use.

BOX-LOCK KEYS

Cabinet 4

AP

Keys to lockout-box locks when those boxes are issued under a safety document.

The Cabinet 4 master key

Cabinet 4 is opened using a controlled common/master cabinet key. Only appropriately authorised APs/SAPs may be issued that key, and they must sign for it. Management status alone does not confer access: anyone without the required AP/SAP authorisation is excluded.

Missing key hook

If an isolation key is removed, a tag is left on its numbered hook indicating where it has gone and why. This is a physical courtesy indicator; the full record is held in the application. A missing key with no valid reconciliation prevents the proposed document issue.

Key distinction:

The master cabinet key opens Cabinet 4. It is not a universal isolation-lock key and must never be used as a bypass of individual isolation or personal-lock control.
