01 / PURPOSE

Why the system exists

Reduce time lost tracing isolation keys, lockout boxes and safety-document recipients on complex projects.

The problem

On large data-centre projects, multiple APs can work across the same electrical system. Keys, lockout boxes and live documents may become difficult to locate. The resulting searches can delay planned work, switching and energisation.

The proposed answer

Standardise the physical locations and identification of locks and keys, then link their custody to the document and the responsible AP/CP in an accessible application.

Two elements working together:

1. A defined, physical LOTO and key-custody system. 2. A supporting digital register for people, assets, keys, lockout boxes and documents.
