06 / DRAWINGS & POIs

Drawing-linked isolation status

Each relevant point on an approved single-line diagram is intended to link to its lock number, current allocation and active document.

What the AP sees

The SLD shows a small circle containing the dedicated lock number next to each relevant switch, circuit breaker or earth switch. The AP selects that point to review its recorded status.

What happens if allocated

If the isolation point is associated with an active safety document, the point is visually struck through or otherwise marked as allocated. Selecting it opens the associated document details, including the recipient CP and contact information.
