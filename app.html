05 / DIGITAL RECORD

How the application works

The app is intended to give authorised users a common view of who holds a document and where keys and boxes are allocated.

CP training and issue

After training, each CP signs for a uniquely numbered personal safety lock. Their name, employer, credentials, authorisation details and contact information are registered. The CP keeps their personal-lock key.

Before issuing a document

The AP checks the POIs against the SLD and recorded key locations. A missing key must be investigated; a document cannot be issued if the necessary key cannot be accounted for.

Safety-document entry

Record document type, work activity, asset, issuing AP, receiving CP, issue date/time, planned duration, lockout box and associated isolation keys. The intended production system shares updates with all authorised users.

Find the responsible person

Authorised users can look up the live document and contact its named CP/AP without searching for paper copies or relying on informal handovers.

Overdue document alerts

When a document exceeds its allocated duration (for example 24 or 36 hours), an alert appears on the opening dashboard, prompting the AP to contact the CP and establish the reason.

Cancellation and reset

After physical locks, keys and the safety document have been reconciled under site rules, the AP updates the app to reflect the cancelled document and the returned items.

Important technical distinction: The reviewed V11 demonstration package does not establish a secured, shared real-time backend; its README describes some document changes as browser-local. Cross-device live synchronisation is therefore a required production capability, not a verified feature of that package.
